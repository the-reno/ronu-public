Ronu / Layout study 01

A quiet space to follow an idea.

This is a working layout example, not a redesign of the article’s content. The same structure can support other subjects.

  1. Chapters. Move between the main sections without losing your place.
  2. Topics. Choose a specific question inside a chapter.
  3. The explanation. Short reading on one side; a useful visual on the other.
  4. Optional depth. Open the mechanism and connections only when you need them.
  5. Continue or focus. Follow the next chapter, search, or reduce the interface.

Try the chapter navigation, topic tabs, diagram steps, expandable notes, search, and Focus view. On a phone, use the menu to open the chapters.
